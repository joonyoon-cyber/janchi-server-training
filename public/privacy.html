Privacy Policy

Janchi POS Trainer · Effective November 13, 2026

The Janchi POS Trainer ("the Trainer") is a training simulator operated by Janchi Korean BBQ & Bar ("Janchi", "we") so that our servers can practice on a copy of our point-of-sale screens. This policy explains what information the Trainer collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Google account information, only if you choose to sign in with Google: your name, email address, Google account identifier and, if Google provides it, your profile picture link.
  • Email and password, if you create an account instead of using Google: your email address and your password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider (Supabase); we cannot see them. We do not send you email.
  • Leaderboard name: the display name shown on the leaderboard. It starts as your Google name and you can change it.
  • Test results: for each Test you finish while signed in, your score, clear time, per-table results, the random seed used to build the Test, and the actions you took on the simulated POS (for example, which buttons you pressed). These are used to rank and, if needed, re-check results.
  • Data stored in your browser: Free Play tables, scenario history and display preferences are saved in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The Trainer never asks for, collects or stores payment card information. All card payments in the Trainer are simulated, and it is not connected to Janchi's real POS, payment processor or kitchen printers.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your Test results.
  • To show the leaderboard: your leaderboard name, best score, clear time, number of Tests and date are visible to everyone signed in to the Trainer.
  • To help Janchi managers follow training progress.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores accounts (emails and hashed passwords), sign-in sessions, leaderboard names and Test results.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google".
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

The Trainer uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to save Free Play and preferences. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and Test results while you use the Trainer. You can ask us to delete your account and results at any time, and we will do so within 30 days.

Your choices

  • You can use Free Play, Scenarios and the Test without signing in; nothing is then sent to us.
  • You can change your leaderboard name on the Leaderboard page.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us (below).

Children

The Trainer is meant for Janchi staff and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
